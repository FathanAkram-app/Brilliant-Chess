What do the labels mean?
Brilliant (!!) — a sound sacrifice that still wins/holds.
Great — the only good move; alternatives were clearly worse.
Best — the engine's top move.
Excellent / Good — fine moves, barely behind the best.
Inaccuracy — a better move existed; small loss.
Mistake — a meaningful error.
Blunder — a serious error (hangs material or allows mate).
